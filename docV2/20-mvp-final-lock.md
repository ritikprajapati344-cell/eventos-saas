EventOS AI V2 – MVP Final Lock (MVPL) v1.0
==========================================

1\. Purpose
-----------

Lock exactly what will be built.

Prevent scope creep.

Protect timeline.

Protect focus.

2\. MVP Mission
===============

Build:

`   AI Event Operator   `

Not:

`   AI Event Empire   `

3\. MVP North Star
==================

User enters:

`   Create a 1500-seat Sunil Grover comedy show in Ahmedabad with ₹50L revenue target.   `

EventOS AI produces:

✅ Event Blueprint

✅ Ticket Strategy

✅ Sponsor Strategy

✅ Timeline

✅ Tasks

✅ Finance Forecast

✅ Risk Report

Then:

`   Approve  ↓  Execute   `

====================

### Screen 1

EventOS AI

(Home)

### Screen 3

Approvals

### Screen 4

History

### Screen 5

Reports

### Screen 6

Settings

Everything else hidden.

6\. MVP Workflow
================

`   Command  ↓  Clarification  ↓  Blueprint  ↓  Approval  ↓  Execution  ↓  History  ↓  Memory   `

Nothing else.

7\. MVP Command Examples
========================

`   Create comedy show  Create corporate event  Create concert  Analyze event  Show event risks  Suggest sponsors  Generate ticket strategy   `

8\. MVP Clarification Questions
===============================

AI may ask:

`   Date?  City?  Budget?  Revenue Target?  Audience Size?   `

Maximum:

`   5 Questions   `

No endless questioning.

9\. MVP Blueprint Output
========================

Must include:

### Executive Summary

### Event Plan

### Ticket Plan

### Sponsor Plan

### Finance Forecast

### Timeline

### Tasks

### Risks

10\. MVP Approval System
========================

Supported:

`   Approve Full Blueprint  Approve Tasks  Approve Timeline  Approve Tickets   `

Only.

11\. MVP Execution
==================

AI can create:

`   Event  Tasks  Timeline  Ticket Categories   `

Only.

12\. MVP Research
=================

Allowed:

`   Sponsor Research  Venue Research  Market Research   `

Only when needed.

13\. MVP Memory
===============

Store:

`   Past Events  Past Blueprints  Past Sponsors  Past Executions   `

Only.

14\. MVP Reports
================

`   Blueprint PDF  Executive Report   `

Only.
