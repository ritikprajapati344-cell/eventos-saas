EventOS AI V2 – Research Specification Document (RSD) v1.0
==========================================================

1\. Purpose
-----------

The Research Engine allows EventOS AI to gather live information from outside the workspace.

Goal:

Combine:

*   Internal Knowledge

*   Workspace Memory

*   Live Research

To produce better event decisions.

2\. Research Philosophy
=======================

Bad AI:

Uses only model knowledge.

Good AI:

Uses memory.

World-Class AI:

Uses memory + live research.

3\. Research Sources
====================

Research may use:

*   Public websites

*   Business directories

*   Event websites

*   Venue websites

*   Sponsor websites

*   Industry reports

*   Search engines

*   Social platforms (future)

Research must never bypass approval rules.

4\. Research Categories
=======================

EventOS AI supports:

### Sponsor Research

Find potential sponsors.

### Venue Research

Find suitable venues.

### Artist Research

Find artist information.

### Market Research

Understand audience demand.

### Pricing Research

Estimate market pricing.

### Competitor Research

Analyze similar events.

### Vendor Research

Find service providers.

### Marketing Research

Find trends and opportunities.

5\. Sponsor Research
====================

User:

Need sponsors for a comedy show in Gandhidham.

Research Engine searches:

*   Local businesses

*   Builders

*   Jewellery brands

*   Hotels

*   Hospitals

*   Automobile dealers

Output:

Sponsor recommendations.

Never auto-create sponsors.

Approval required.

6\. Venue Research
==================

User:

Suggest venues for 1500 people in Ahmedabad.

Research Engine returns:

*   Venue options

*   Capacity

*   Location

*   Suitability

Output:

Venue recommendations.

7\. Artist Research
===================

User:

I want Sunil Grover.

Research Engine gathers:

*   Public artist information

*   Show format

*   Audience profile

*   Market positioning

Output:

Artist planning context.

8\. Market Research
===================

Market opportunity report.

Lessons and benchmarks.

12\. Marketing Research
=======================

Marketing recommendations.

13\. Research Workflow
======================

User Command

↓

Research Request

↓

Research Engine

↓

Source Collection

↓

Validation

↓

Summary Generation

↓

Recommendation

↓

Approval (if needed)

14\. Research Confidence
========================

Every result gets confidence.

Low

Medium

High

Very High

Users should know reliability.

15\. Research Validation
========================

Research results should be:

*   Source-backed

*   Traceable

*   Explainable

AI should show:

Why this recommendation exists.

16\. Research Output Format
===========================

Every research response contains:

Executive Summary

Key Findings

Opportunities

Risks

Recommendations

Sources Used

Confidence Score

17\. Research Safety Rules
==========================

Research cannot:

*   Create records

*   Modify records

*   Send messages

*   Approve actions

Research produces recommendations only.

18\. Research Memory
====================

Research findings may become memory only after:

*   User approval

*   Event execution

*   Verified outcomes

Unverified research should not become permanent memory.

19\. Research Expiry
====================

Research becomes stale.

Examples:

Sponsor lists

Venue pricing

Market trends

Old research receives lower confidence over time.

20\. Research Engine North Star
===============================

When users ask:

"Find sponsors in Gandhidham"

The system should combine:

Workspace Memory

Knowledge Engine

Live Research

To produce better recommendations than generic AI.
